# NOVA V5 / portfolio edition

Audience: Upwork clients assessing interactive product design and front-end delivery.
Goal: explore a real-time product, understand the craft, and leave with a useful project brief.

Direction: warm ivory, near-black and restrained copper; large sans headlines with editorial serif accents; generous product space; no invented sales results or fake checkout. NOVA remains an independent concept. Sony specifications remain clearly attributed as a content reference and never as measurements of the supplied model.

Architecture: retain the modular vanilla JavaScript / Three.js application, one GLTF and one canvas. Timeline owns authored motion; fold and inspection controllers own direct manipulation; composer combines them; render adapter renders; product UI owns semantic controls and dialogs.

V5 priorities:
- Product/copy separation on desktop and 360/390/430px mobile.
- Concise benefit-led copy, clear reference disclosure, usable dark controls.
- Smooth reset, single-owner inspection spring, reduced-motion control, hidden-tab suspension.
- Keyboard navigation, inert inactive scenes, dialog focus containment and restoration.
- Actual model render for fallback; metadata and share image.
- Shorter, credible case study and platform-safe project-brief CTA.

Acceptance: source contracts; browser journeys at desktop, tablet and mobile; full product bounds; no active-copy overlap; preset/reset/reverse-scroll, fold, dialogs, mobile nav, reduced motion, WebGL/model failure; screenshot review; production bundle then V5-only publish.
