Product workflow

A clear path from store creation to configured operations.

The workflow below describes what EZComo controls directly and where an external provider, merchant configuration, entitlement, DNS, or verification step still matters.

1. Create the store

Choose a storefront structure, create the merchant store, and continue into the dashboard. Store creation is subject to the active/selectable plan catalog.

2. Add the selling details

Set up products or business content, navigation, storefront sections, checkout-facing information, and the settings needed for your business model.

3. Configure operations

Configure payment, courier, notification, domain, and other operational settings that are available to the store. External connections keep an explicit readiness state.

How external capabilities are represented

Payment connections

EZComo distinguishes not configured, configured, verified, and needs-attention states. A provider is not called connected just because credentials or display settings exist.

Courier workflows

Booking, status, label, notification, or other courier behavior is used only where the enabled provider and its implemented capabilities support that action.

Custom domains

Domain tooling is plan/feature gated and still requires hostname, DNS, and verification steps before a custom hostname can serve the storefront.

Background operations

Notification and background-job processing are handled by product workflows; their delivery still depends on the configured channel or external service.
