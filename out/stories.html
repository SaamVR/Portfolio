Illustrative examples — not testimonials

Example merchant workflows and storefront scenarios.

These scenarios demonstrate ways EZComo can be configured. They do not represent named customers, star ratings, verified reviews, revenue claims, launch-time claims, or measured merchant outcomes.

Fashion catalog example

Illustrative setup: a fashion merchant can start from a catalog-oriented template, organize collections, add products, and adjust storefront sections and theme settings.

Beauty storefront example

Illustrative setup: a beauty store can combine product discovery, brand content, trust information, checkout details, and merchant-managed promotions.

Electronics operations example

Illustrative setup: an electronics merchant can structure product information, manage catalog and order workflows, and configure available payment or delivery connections.

Food ordering example

Illustrative setup: a food business can use a menu-oriented storefront structure, organize items, publish ordering information, and manage incoming commerce activity.
