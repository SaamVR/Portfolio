# NOVA — portfolio listing

**Project title:** NOVA — Interactive 3D Product Website

**Role:** Art direction, UI/UX design, motion design and front-end development

**Description:**
I designed and developed an interactive headphone website that brings a product story into the browser. Visitors can explore design details, fold the headphone and inspect it from different angles. The experience combines responsive layouts, scroll-driven Three.js animation, direct manipulation, keyboard controls and reduced-motion support. A static product view keeps the story usable when 3D is unavailable. NOVA is an independent concept project built with a supplied animated 3D asset; hardware specifications are clearly attributed to a published reference.

**Skills:** Web Design · Front-End Development · Three.js · Interaction Design · Animation
