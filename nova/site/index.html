on your terms.

Move between two conceptual noise-control behaviors: one prioritizes isolation, while the other keeps awareness of the surrounding space.

NOVA / CONCEPT PRODUCT FACTS

Concept product facts

What the demo
actually represents.

Format
Over-ear wireless headphone concept with an articulated folding form.
Listening profiles
Spatial, Focus and Ambient are visual concept profiles—not measured acoustic claims.
Noise control
Adaptive and Transparency are conceptual interaction states demonstrated through the environment.
Controls
The 3D model exposes a physical earcup control surface through a product hotspot.
Multipoint
Multipoint is part of the concept feature set; protocol, compatibility and implementation are not specified.
Dimensions / weight / battery
Not specified in this fictional concept. No invented performance numbers are presented.
Is NOVA a real product?

No. NOVA is a fictional product used to demonstrate an interactive product-launch experience.

Are the acoustic claims measured?

No. The site demonstrates interface and storytelling concepts, not verified acoustic or laboratory performance.

What is genuinely interactive?

The live 3D model, authored camera and lighting, folding state, named inspection views, hotspots and responsive choreography run in the browser.
