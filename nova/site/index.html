Adaptive represents focused isolation. Transparency represents awareness and a more open connection to the environment.

01

Long-haul travel

The benchmark class spans roughly 254–293 g and 30–60 hours of advertised battery life, making weight, clamping comfort and case geometry as important as headline battery numbers.

02

Phone + laptop

Multipoint is normal in this tier. Bose documents two active devices; Sennheiser documents two active connections; Sony lists multipoint support.

03

Wired fallback

Sony retains a stereo mini jack, Bose combines AUX with USB-C audio, and Sennheiser supports analog plus USB-C streaming—useful when Bluetooth is unavailable or latency matters.

04

Quick charge

Useful recovery ranges from Sony's 3-minute USB-PD top-up to Bose's 15-minute charge and Sennheiser's 5-minute charge. Charging behavior is a daily-use feature, not a footnote.
