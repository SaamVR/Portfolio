DESIGN

Daily listening contexts

One headset.
Three situations.

Commute, Office and Travel ask for different levels of immersion and awareness. The interaction states below communicate those everyday priorities without pretending this design study has completed acoustic lab validation.

Immersive prioritizes a wide, cinematic presentation. Focus settles the environment for concentrated Office work. Aware keeps more visual openness for stations, gates and street movement.

Noise control

Quiet when needed.
Aware when it matters.

For Commute and Office use, the primary control should be understandable without opening an app: isolate the environment or let important surroundings back in.

ANC prioritizes isolation for transit and focused work. Aware prioritizes announcements, conversation and street context.
