NOVA / REFERENCE SPECIFICATIONS

Reference hardware benchmark

The details.
All together.

Hardware benchmark: Sony WH-1000XM6. NOVA is an independent product design. These specifications describe the Sony benchmark, rather than measured NOVA hardware. There is no affiliation with Sony.

Weight
Approx. 254 g / 8.96 oz
Driver
30 mm / 1.18 in dynamic driver / closed over-ear format
Wireless
Bluetooth 5.3 / effective range approx. 10 m / 32.81 ft
Codecs
SBC / AAC / LDAC / LC3
Battery
AAC/SBC/LC3: up to 30 hrs NC on / 40 hrs NC off. LDAC: up to 26 hrs NC on / 36 hrs NC off.
Charge time
Approx. 3.5 hrs via USB
Connections
Multipoint: 2 devices / 3.5 mm Stereo Mini Jack / passive operation supported
Controls
Touch sensor panel for playback, volume and calls; physical NC/AMB button for noise-control mode.
Included
Carrying case, connection cable and USB cable in the reference package
How does multipoint help day to day?

Multipoint lets the reference headset stay connected to two devices, so you can switch between a phone and laptop without repeating pairing each time.

Can it be used when wireless is inconvenient?

Yes. A supplied 3.5 mm headphone cable supports wired listening. Bluetooth is unavailable while the supplied headphone cable is connected; passive playback remains available with the headset powered off, while Noise Canceling and Ambient Sound require power.

How do the physical controls work?

The reference uses a Touch sensor panel on the right earcup: double-tap for play/pause, swipe forward/back for tracks and swipe up/down for volume. A separate NC/AMB button changes noise-control mode.

What battery figure is being used?

AAC, SBC and LC3 reach up to 30 hrs with Noise Canceling on and 40 hrs off. LDAC has a lower official maximum: up to 26 hrs NC on and 36 hrs off.
