DESIGN

Immersive audio

Your space becomes
the soundstage.

40 mm dynamic drivers pair with SBC, AAC, LDAC and LC3 playback. Choose a wider Spatial presentation, a tighter Focus profile or an Ambient profile that keeps the soundstage open.

Spatial opens the presentation for a wider, more immersive listening feel. Focus reduces surrounding motion. Ambient keeps the visual field open to the world around you.

Adaptive listening

Silence,
on your terms.

Hybrid adaptive ANC is designed to react to changing surroundings, while Transparency keeps outside sound available when awareness matters.

Adaptive represents focused isolation. Transparency represents awareness and a more open connection to the environment.

Fold the earcups inward for the hard carry case, then open NOVA back into its listening form. The 260 g design uses aluminum-reinforced hinge points and memory-foam cushions for everyday travel.

Can I use NOVA wired?

Yes. The design specification includes USB-C digital audio and a 3.5 mm analog input for devices where Bluetooth is not the right fit.

Can NOVA stay connected to two devices?

Yes. Two-device multipoint is part of the design specification, so a laptop and phone can remain actively connected.

How long does the battery last?

The engineering target is up to 30 h with ANC enabled and 40 h with ANC off, with a 10 min quick charge targeting up to 5 h playback.

Which Bluetooth codecs are supported?

SBC, AAC, LDAC and LC3 are included in the V3 connectivity target.
