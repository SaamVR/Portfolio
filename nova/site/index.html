Noise control / awareness

Quiet when
you need it.

Use active noise canceling for travel or focused work, then switch to Ambient Sound when voices, announcements or traffic need to remain audible.

Noise Canceling pairs with the 30-hour NC-on reference battery target. Ambient Sound keeps useful surroundings in the mix.

How does multipoint help day to day?

Multipoint lets the reference headset stay connected to two devices, so you can switch between a phone and laptop without repeating pairing each time.

Can it be used when wireless is inconvenient?

Yes. The reference specification includes a Stereo Mini Jack and passive operation for wired passive playback.

What battery figure is being used?

The real reference lists up to 30 hrs with noise canceling on and 40 hrs with noise canceling off.
