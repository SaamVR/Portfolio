NOVA / Interactive product experience

Designed like a launch.
Built like a product.

A fictional premium headphone campaign built around a live animated 3D asset—not a pre-rendered product video. The interface, camera, product motion and responsive behavior are all authored as one system.

01 / Role

Role

Creative direction, interaction design, front-end prototyping and real-time 3D choreography across desktop, tablet and mobile.

02 / Challenge

Challenge

Make one GLTF feel like a premium product campaign without hiding behind pre-rendered video, while keeping direct interactions legible and responsive.

03 / Build

What I built

A scroll-driven Three.js experience with authored camera choreography, state-based lighting, skinned-surface hotspots, bounded inspection and responsive composition rules.

04 / Result

Result

A portfolio piece that behaves like a launch experience first, then reveals the implementation decisions and technical constraints behind the presentation.

Runtime anatomy

The same headphone explored above runs from the live skinned GLTF and its authored source animation. Product framing, lighting and interaction state are composed at runtime.
