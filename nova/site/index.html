DESIGN

Immersive audio

Your space becomes
the soundstage.

Bose Immersive Audio spatializes playback beyond the earcups, while Cinema Mode widens the soundstage and emphasizes dialogue for video, podcasts and spoken-word content.

Immersion demonstrates the expanded spatial field. Cinema emphasizes a forward soundstage and dialogue; Standard settles the visual field for conventional stereo listening.

Adaptive listening

Silence,
on your terms.

Quiet Mode prioritizes noise cancellation. Aware Mode keeps environmental sound present, while ActiveSense can automatically smooth sudden noise spikes such as traffic, appliances or a passing train.

Quiet Mode focuses the presentation inward. Aware Mode opens the visual field to reflect environmental awareness.

Articulated form

Made to move.

Fold the earcups inward for the included carry case. When you take the headphones off, on-head detection can pause playback; laying them flat or folding them away can move them toward low-power standby.

Hold the folded pose long enough to read the hinge, then reopen it when you are ready.

QC ULTRA / OFFICIAL SPECIFICATIONS

Official specifications

QuietComfort Ultra
2nd Gen.

Battery
30 hours in Quiet/Aware; 23 hours with Immersive Audio; up to 45 hours with noise cancellation off.
Charging
3-hour full charge. 15-minute charge / up to 3 hours of playback (up to 2 hours with Immersive Audio).
Wireless
Bluetooth 5.4, up to 33 ft range; Multipoint connects to two devices at once.
USB-C audio
16-bit / 44.1 or 48 kHz USB-C audio from compatible source files; listening while charging is supported.
Wired audio
3.5 mm to 2.5 mm audio cable included.
Weight / fit
0.583 lb / approx. 264 g. Around-ear circumaural fit with adjustable headband and removable cushions.
Materials
Plastic, aluminum and Protein leather; removable protein-leather ear cushions.
Listening
Quiet Mode, Aware Mode with ActiveSense, Bose Immersive Audio, Cinema Mode and CustomTune.
